# PROOF AUDIT — evidence levels — 2026-10-18

## Evidence levels
| Level | Meaning | Can be shown as |
|---|---|---|
| **PUBLICLY VERIFIED** | Anyone can check it right now (public repo, CI badge, live site). | fact, with a link |
| **OWNER-ATTESTED** | True by the owner's direct daily experience, not independently checkable. | first-person claim, labeled |
| **DEMONSTRATION** | Runs on the site as a live demo; shows the mechanism, not production usage. | interactive artifact |
| **ARCHIVED** | Real past work, no longer operating. Presented as history, never as current capability. | dated archive entry |

## Rules
1. **No client logos, no client outcomes.** None exist to show; inventing or implying any is forbidden. Credibility comes from mechanisms and public artifacts, not a logo wall.
2. **No employer-internal detail.** Nothing derived from the owner's employer's systems, data, or internals — no matter how illustrative.
3. **Negative results are preserved.** Smart Trader's frozen feed, failed experiments, and explicit "not promised" lists stay visible. Showing what stopped and why is the credibility strategy, not a liability.
4. Every claim on the site carries its evidence level; a visitor should never have to guess whether something is verifiable, attested, a demo, or archived.
